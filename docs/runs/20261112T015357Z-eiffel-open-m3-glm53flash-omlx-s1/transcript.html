### Cel shading & ink
- `MeshToonMaterial` with a 3-step nearest-filtered gradient map → hard light bands, no gradients.
- **Outlines**: inverted-hull shader (back-face normal expansion) on structures/trees, plus grown shells for the instanced beams — ink joints form naturally where members meet.
- Sky is a custom shader with hard quantized bands and stepped sun-glow rings.

### Real shadows
One directional sun/moon with a 2048px shadow map casts the tower's shadow across striped Champ-de-Mars lawns — long and dramatic at dusk, soft and blue at night.

### Day / Dusk / Night
- **Day**: bright cyan banded sky, vivid greens, cream skyline.
- **Dusk**: red/amber banded sky, low sun, long shadows, windows beginning to glow.
- **Night**: deep indigo, stars, moonlight, warm golden floodlights + emissive iron so the tower glows instead of going silhouette, lit windows, lamp pools, fireflies.
- Everything cross-fades over ~1.5 s via a blended palette engine; opens directly in a mode via `?tod=day|dusk|night` (default `day`), and the buttons update the URL.

### Life & surroundings
Striped lawns, gravel paths, plaza, a Seine with a bridge and a drifting barge, a little Parisian skyline with per-instance windows that light up at night, low-poly toon trees, lamp posts, drifting clouds, circling birds, and a gently auto-rotating camera that hands control to you on first drag (orbit + zoom, damped).
