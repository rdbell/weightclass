Key ideas

  • f7 is defended only by the king — the classic fast-mate target.
  • Two attackers beat one defender: bishop + queen strike together.
  • 3...Nf6 attacks the queen but abandons f7 — the knight arrives too late.
  • Best defense: spot the threat early, keep f7 protected, develop with purpose.
