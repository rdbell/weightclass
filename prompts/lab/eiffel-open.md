Build a stylized, cel-shaded scene of the Eiffel Tower, in the bright, toon-shaded look of a cartoon adventure game.

Requirements:

- The tower should be recognizable: its curved, tapering profile, open iron lattice, platforms and antenna.
- Cel shading with hard light bands, not smooth gradients, and ink-style outlines.
- Real cast shadows that fit the style.
- A Day / Dusk / Night toggle that changes the lighting and colours of the whole scene. Make the night scene readable, not a silhouette. The page also opens directly in a mode via `?tod=day`, `?tod=dusk` or `?tod=night`, defaulting to day.
- The viewer can orbit and zoom around the tower.

Everything else is your call: the tower's surroundings, the palette, the camera, how you build the shading, and any extra detail or life you think makes the scene better. Aim for something you would be proud to show.
